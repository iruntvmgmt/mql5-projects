# D032 — Standalone Synthetic Screening

Phase 3 of the D030–D035 six-family shadow research program. Scope: run
all six D031 families' real candidate stream through one shared, frozen
simulator, apply the handoff's frozen screening gates, and produce a
promotion decision. **No family definitions, thresholds, or gates were
changed based on these results** — frozen before inspection, per the
handoff's anti-overfitting rules.

Branch: `feature/d030-six-family-shadow-research`, continuing from D031's
certification (`d1ea6d0aa7aaabb50ade02d37d9019e122022dfc`).

Methodology, inputs, and the simulator's frozen assumptions:
`Tools/D032/README.md`. All figures below are from `Tools/D032/out/`,
generated by `Tools/D032/simulate_and_screen.py` against **real** data —
the 3,234 candidates D031's own certification Config B run actually
produced over the full certified P4 window (2025.03.03–2026.07.24, XAUUSD
M5), not a fresh synthetic sample.

## A real bug found and fixed while building this

`MSZZ_SixFamilyResearchJournal.csv`'s `structural_context` field embeds
its own `;`-separated sub-fields, colliding with the CSV's own `;`
delimiter (MQL5's `FileWrite` doesn't escape delimiters inside a field).
All six `Research/Families/*.mqh` files switched to `|` internally
(matching this codebase's existing `origin_id`/`event_id` convention).
Recompiled clean (0 errors, 0 warnings) on the isolated instance. This
analysis itself works around the already-generated (real) Config B
evidence by reconstructing correct field boundaries — see
`Tools/D032/simulate_and_screen.py`'s `load_candidates()` docstring — no
new backtest was needed to produce the numbers below.

## Per-family verdicts and why

### 1200 Session Sweep Reversal — passes all mandatory gates, promotion case is real but not overwhelming

822 trades (comfortably over the 100-trade minimum), PF 1.079 (>1.05),
positive expectancy in development (0.044), validation (0.113), **and**
holdout (0.013), top-3 exclusion still positive, best-quarter exclusion
still positive. Long (0.081) and short (0.028) both positive — no
one-direction dependency. This is the strongest empirical result of the
six.

It does **not** clear the handoff's *optional* promotion preference
(PF ≥ 1.15 — SSR sits at 1.079). Its case for promotion rests on the
other listed factor: signal uniqueness / low correlation, not raw PF.

**Redundancy check** (`Tools/D032/out/redundancy_overlap.csv`), the
question the family's own definition explicitly flags: SSR's trade
windows overlap the existing **SweepReclaim (1050)** book only 32 times
out of 822 SSR trades (~3.9%) — genuinely low, despite both sharing a
"sweep and reclaim" hypothesis at the conceptual level. SSR's narrower
Asia-session-specific trigger is empirically **not** redundant with the
general-purpose SweepReclaim book. (Overlap with FastMedConfluence is
higher, 329/822 ≈ 40%, but that's just co-occurrence in time between two
unrelated hypotheses, not a redundancy signal in the causal sense.)

A real cost of its own design showed up in simulation: 2,650 raw
candidates were generated, but 1,828 (69%) were rejected by the
frozen "no same-family stacking" rule — Asia-session sweeps cluster,
and the family's own 6-bar reclaim window is short relative to how often
qualifying excursions recur. This isn't a defect (the simulator's
one-position-per-family rule is exactly what the handoff requires), but
it means the *addressable* opportunity is meaningfully smaller than the
raw candidate count suggests.

### 1201 Momentum Continuation — zero trades, real and unresolved

The family D030 rated **highest priority** — because it directly targets
P4's largest, most stable loss cluster — produced **zero** candidates
over 17 real months of XAUUSD M5 data, despite D031's own synthetic
tests (Gate 3) proving its logic fires correctly under controlled
conditions. Its frozen arm condition requires
`directional_efficiency>=0.55` **and** `fast_swing_amplitude_r>=1.5`
**and** fast/medium alignment simultaneously — apparently a combination
this instrument/timeframe's real regime distribution essentially never
produces at the same time. **Not loosened here** — that would be exactly
the "tune thresholds after seeing results" the handoff prohibits in this
same study. Classified `REDESIGN_REQUIRED`, not `REJECTED`: the
underlying hypothesis (displacement + pause continuation) is untested,
not disproven — only this specific frozen operationalization of it is.

### 1202 Break-Retest Continuation — rejected

PF 0.72, negative expectancy in all three splits, top-3 and best-quarter
exclusion both still negative (i.e. losses are not concentrated in a few
trades or one period — this is a broad, structural negative edge, not
noise). Also the highest P4-overlap of the six (64.7% of its trades
coincide with an open P4 position) — redundant *and* unprofitable.
`REJECTED`.

### 1203 Compression Breakout (Research) — close miss, worth a second look later

PF 1.155 (would clear the optional 1.15 preference), positive total
expectancy (+25.0R), positive in development (0.123) and holdout (0.231)
— but **validation is negative** (-0.050, 60 trades), which fails the
mandatory "positive validation expectancy" gate outright. Long trades
strongly positive (0.321), short trades negative (-0.069) — a genuine
one-direction asymmetry worth noting, though not itself a separate
mandatory-gate failure here. Top-3 and best-quarter exclusion both still
positive. This is the second-best result of the six and the only
mandatory-gate failure that comes from a single split rather than an
outright negative edge. Classified `RESEARCH_ONLY`, not `REJECTED` — a
future study (not this one, and not by loosening today's frozen
definition) could examine whether the validation-window shortfall is
structural or a period-specific effect.

### 1204 Trend Pullback — inconclusive, sample too thin to score

Only 5 resolved trades (12 raw candidates, 7 rejected for same-family
stacking). Far below the 100-trade minimum; the positive PF (1.333) and
expectancy (0.200) shown are not statistically meaningful at n=5 and
must not be read as a result. D030 already flagged this family as
"secondary priority" with real complementary potential but overlap risk
against Momentum Continuation — that comparison cannot be made either
(Momentum Continuation had zero trades). `RESEARCH_ONLY` /
insufficient-sample, not `REJECTED`.

### 1205 Range Rotation — rejected

PF 0.855, negative expectancy overall and in both validation (-0.304)
and holdout (-0.379); development alone was marginally positive (0.052).
Top-3 and best-quarter exclusion both negative — broad, not
concentration-driven. Shortest average holding time of the six (4.0 bars
vs 17-77 for the others) — consistent with its reclaim-based trigger
resolving quickly, but the frozen `MIN_TARGET_R=0.5` reward-to-risk floor
evidently wasn't enough to produce a positive edge here. `REJECTED`.
Lowest D031 implementation priority is reconfirmed by this result, not
contradicted by it.

## Redundancy analysis (cross-family)

Pairwise trade-window overlap between the six families themselves is
generally modest relative to trade counts (e.g. SSR vs Compression
Breakout: 104/822 ≈ 12.6%; SSR vs Break-Retest: 48/822 ≈ 5.8%; most other
pairs single digits or near-zero) — full table:
`Tools/D032/out/redundancy_overlap.csv`. No pair shows the kind of
"most signals are the same event" pattern that would indicate two
families are actually re-detecting one phenomenon.

## Promotion decision

**Promote: Session Sweep Reversal (1200) only.**

The handoff caps promotion at three, defaults to two, and requires a
third to demonstrate "clear diversification" — but only one family
actually cleared the mandatory gate battery here. Promoting a second or
third family that failed a mandatory gate (regardless of how close —
Compression Breakout's single-split miss included) would be exactly the
"lower gates after results" the handoff prohibits. Session Sweep
Reversal:

- passes every mandatory gate (100+ trades, positive total/dev/val/holdout
  expectancy, PF>1.05, top-3 and best-quarter exclusion still positive);
- is empirically low-correlation with the existing SweepReclaim book
  despite the conceptual similarity (3.9% trade-window overlap);
- shows no one-direction dependency;
- does not clear the *optional* PF≥1.15 preference, so this is a
  standalone-robustness-driven promotion, not a "clearly excellent
  standalone result" one — D033's actual portfolio test (does adding it
  to P4 improve PF/DD, not just standalone) is the real test, exactly as
  the handoff intends ("promote narrowly at the execution level").

## Data limitations

- Momentum Continuation and Trend Pullback's near-empty candidate counts
  mean this study cannot say anything about whether their *hypotheses*
  are sound — only that these specific frozen operationalizations rarely
  or never fire on XAUUSD M5 over this window.
- The simulator's "no same-family stacking" rule materially shrinks the
  addressable trade count for high-frequency-signal families (most
  visibly Session Sweep Reversal, 69% of raw candidates rejected) — this
  is the frozen assumption working as intended, not a data gap, but it
  means standalone PF is measured on the *achievable* trade stream, not
  the raw signal-generation rate.
- MFE/MAE and holding time are now directly measurable (unlike D030,
  which lacked bar-level portfolio-trade MFE/MAE) because this
  simulation walks real M5 bars per trade — see `avg_mfe_r`/`avg_mae_r`/
  `avg_holding_bars` columns in `screening_summary.csv`.
